## Engine-wide issues (fix once, many cards benefit)

1. **Triggered abilities never receive targets.** `TriggerManager` queues every trigger with `addAbility(source, controller, def)` (empty target list), and `GameEngine::chooseTargetsAtFinalize` returns early for triggers, so `getTargetRequirements()` is never consulted for them. Every `onTrigger` that reads `targets[0]` without `pickTarget` silently does nothing. 27 cards match mechanically:

   `0278_navori_fighting_pit.cpp`, `0535_veiled_temple.cpp`, `0763_amateur_recital.cpp`, `0063_spirit_s_refuge.cpp`, `0695_blast_cone.cpp`, `0016_dangerous_duo.cpp`, `0051_solari_shieldbearer.cpp`, `0076_yasuo_remorseful.cpp`, `0082_whiteflame_protector.cpp`, `0092_riptide_rex.cpp`, `0097_blastcone_fae.cpp`, `0130_crackshot_corsair.cpp`, `0132_first_mate.cpp`, `0188_zaunite_bouncer.cpp`, `0191_maddened_marauder.cpp`, `0199_tideturner.cpp`, `0234_harnessed_dragon.cpp`, `0238_leona_determined.cpp`, `0330_gem_jammer.cpp`, `0361_ribbon_dancer.cpp`, `0384_aspiring_engineer.cpp`, `0453_beast_below.cpp`, `0479_sandshifter.cpp`, `0539_ahri_inquisitive.cpp`, `0589_inviolus_vox.cpp`, `0618_yuumi_magical_cat.cpp`, `0738_vi_peacekeeper.cpp`

   Fix options: have the finalize pass fill `targets` from `getTargetRequirements()` for triggers too, or migrate these cards to `pickTarget` with `kFinalizeTrigger`.

2. **Keywords in `def()` that the text only mentions.** Several units carry a keyword their text grants to *others* or only conditionally ("give it [Temporary]", "Your Mechs have [Deflect]", "[Level 6] I have [Ganking]"). Since `hasKeyword()` reads base keywords, the unit always has it. Worst case is `Temporary`: Sprite Mother, Trevor Snoozebottom, Petal Pixie, Lillia Fae Fawn, Sprite Queen and LeBlanc die at their controller's next Beginning Phase. 33 unit/keyword pairs to adjudicate (mechanical sweep, false positives removed by hand):

   `0015_captain_farron.cpp` (Assault), `0018_noxus_saboteur.cpp` (Hidden), `0106_sprite_mother.cpp` (Temporary), `0107_ava_achiever.cpp` (Hidden), `0157_udyr_wildman.cpp` (Ganking), `0167_ember_monk.cpp` (Hidden), `0232_fiora_victorious.cpp` (Deflect), `0232_fiora_victorious.cpp` (Ganking), `0232_fiora_victorious.cpp` (Shield), `0330_gem_jammer.cpp` (Ganking), `0349_rumble_hotheaded.cpp` (Assault), `0388_forecaster.cpp` (Vision), `0394_breakneck_mech.cpp` (Deflect), `0394_breakneck_mech.cpp` (Ganking), `0452_ancient_warmonger.cpp` (Assault), `0464_sivir_mercenary.cpp` (Ganking), `0574_lord_broadmane.cpp` (Assault), `0595_frisky_hunter.cpp` (Deflect), `0609_mosstomper.cpp` (Deflect), `0610_trevor_snoozebottom.cpp` (Temporary), `0618_yuumi_magical_cat.cpp` (Tank), `0620_lillia_protector_of_dreams.cpp` (Tank), `0633_chakram_dancer.cpp` (Shield), `0637_gustwalker.cpp` (Ganking), `0638_petal_pixie.cpp` (Temporary), `0644_lillia_fae_fawn.cpp` (Temporary), `0646_sprite_queen.cpp` (Temporary), `0652_leblanc_everywhere_at_once.cpp` (Temporary), `0675_master_yi_tempered.cpp` (Deflect), `0675_master_yi_tempered.cpp` (Ganking), `0688_megatusk.cpp` (Ganking), `0715_carrion_dredger.cpp` (Deflect), `0722_ultrasoft_poro.cpp` (Deflect)

3. **State changed before `pickTarget`/`pickMode` suspends.** On resume the card re-runs from the top and repeats the side effect (double banish/replay, double XP spend, double gear kill). Seen in Arcane Shift, Conscription, Stare Down, Jayce Man of Progress, Twisted Fate Gambler. Same root as the CLAUDE.md rule "nothing before `finalizingChoices` may change state", applied to resolve-time choices.

4. **`EffectExecutor::buffUnit` stacks buffs.** It adds a buff even when the unit already has one (CR 427.1.b.1 says it shouldn't). Affects Karma Channeler, Adaptatron, Pit Rookie, Overt Operation and every "[Buff]" card that doesn't guard it.

5. **`parseRepeatCost` can't read `[Repeat] — <cost>`.** The em-dash form (Curtain Call, Square Up) yields an invalid cost, so the repeat is never offered and, for Square Up, the discard is charged every cast.

6. **Weaponmaster base class.** `weaponmaster_base.h` (and its hand copies on Yone, Armed Assailant, Combat Chef, Veteran Poro, Ornn, Lucian) always attaches the first Equipment for free, with no "may" and no player choice. Text: optional, Equip cost reduced by [A]. Jax, Unrelenting shows the correct pattern.

7. **`WhenIBecomeExhausted` (new for Radiance).** `exhaustObject` emits the event even when the unit was already exhausted, giving Riven, Broken Blade and Reckless Innovator free triggers; three recall-instead-of-die paths set `is_exhausted` directly and emit nothing. No tests exist for 957, 959, 960, 961 or this trigger.

8. **Smaller shared gaps.** `requiresLevel()/levelThreshold()/requiresLegion()` are never read by the engine; Hunt is per-card; `PlayerState::xp_gained_this_turn` is never incremented; legends never fire `WhenAnEnemyUnitDies` (no location); [Deflect] cost is tracked but not charged (already noted in `set-update-2026-10.md`).
